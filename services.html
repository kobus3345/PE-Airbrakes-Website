Our Services

Full-Spectrum Brake Services

Every service is performed by qualified technicians using quality parts. We specialise exclusively in heavy vehicle brake systems.

Air Brake System Repair

Full diagnostics and repair of pneumatic air brake systems including valves, chambers, slack adjusters, and airlines.

  • Air dryer service
  • Relay and control valves
  • Brake chambers
  • Slack adjusters

Brake Drum and Disc Machining

On-site machining and replacement of brake drums and discs to manufacturer specifications.

  • Drum machining
  • Disc resurfacing
  • Hub and drum assemblies
  • Wheel bearing inspection

Brake Pad and Lining Replacement

Quality brake pads and linings for all heavy vehicle applications.

  • Brake pads and linings
  • Riveted and bonded linings
  • Premium friction materials
  • All truck makes and models

ABS and Electronic Diagnostics

Specialised equipment to identify faults in ABS, EBS, and associated control modules.

  • ABS fault code reading
  • Wheel speed sensor testing
  • EBS module diagnosis
  • Software updates

Air Compressor Overhaul

Complete rebuild and replacement of air compressors that supply safe operating pressure.

  • Compressor rebuild kits
  • Valve plate replacement
  • Pressure testing

Brake System Safety Inspections

Comprehensive pre-roadworthy and scheduled inspections for RTMS compliance.

  • Full brake balance test
  • Brake efficiency report
  • Pre-roadworthy certification
  • Written inspection report
