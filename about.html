About PE Airbrakes

Two Decades of Keeping Eastern Cape Trucks Rolling Safely

PE Airbrakes was founded in Port Elizabeth in 2004 by Gerhard Pietersen, a master technician with a simple vision: a brake specialist workshop that treats every truck like it is the only one in the yard.

We have grown from a small two-bay workshop to a fully equipped specialist facility with eight technicians and the ability to service everything from a two-axle rigid to a B-train interlink.

We work with transport companies, fleet operators, owner-drivers, and logistics firms across the Eastern Cape.
